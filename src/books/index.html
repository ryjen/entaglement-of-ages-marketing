Entanglement of Ages

Four ages. Four forms of authority.

Across deep history and distant futures, humanity repeatedly discovers something real—strength, law, intelligence, selection—and then mistakes possession of it for the right to define another person.

Editorial status

The series remains in development.

Books I-III have beta manuscripts. Book IV, The Age of Forms, is currently in story-foundation and architecture development.

Editors, beta readers, subject-matter readers, and other contributors are welcome to register interest without posting manuscript text, spoilers, unpublished story material, raw feedback, or personal contact information.
