In development

A civilization that can measure almost everything

After a catastrophic break in human continuity, fragments of advanced infrastructure survive into a society that organizes more and more of life around visibility, reputation, selection, desirability, and measurable forms of value.

A spiritual student who has always sensed something wrong finally receives language for the pattern. His realization is not that the wrong people are winning. It is that a system built around comparative human worth cannot provide peace even to its winners.

Inheritance

Advanced systems persist after the institutions and historical context that once bounded them have fractured.

Conflict

Choice, attraction, economics, media, and reputation can produce coercive social outcomes without a single visible coercer.

Question

What remains of human value when every visible form can be measured, compared, ranked, and selected?
